About the cakery

Made for moments worth remembering.

At The Cakery, we believe every celebration deserves something special. As a premium cake shop in Kannur, we create beautifully crafted cakes for birthdays, weddings, anniversaries, and every memorable occasion.

From custom-designed cakes to classic favourites, every creation is made using quality ingredients and expert craftsmanship.

Beyond cakes, we also offer a variety of freshly prepared snacks, pastries, brownies, cupcakes, and bakery treats that are perfect for everyday indulgence and special gatherings.

The Cakery proudly operates under The Borma & Co, bringing a commitment to quality, creativity, and exceptional customer experience in every product we serve.
